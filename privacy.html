Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Northbound Digital ("we," "us") provides fixed-price technical delivery services: website builds, cloud/server setup, and workflow automation. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

This website does not use cookies, analytics, or any tracking scripts. We only receive information you choose to send us directly — for example, by emailing us or replying to an email from us. That typically includes your name, email address, and whatever you tell us about your project.

For outreach emails we send (see below), we use publicly available business contact information — the email address a business itself has published on its own website — nothing scraped from private sources or purchased from a third-party list.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry and, if you choose to proceed, to scope, deliver, and invoice a project.
  • To send a one-time, individually-written outreach email if we believe our services may be relevant to your business — never automated bulk/mail-merge sending, and never repeated after you opt out.
  • We keep an internal record of which businesses we've already contacted, solely to avoid contacting the same business twice.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties.
  • We do not use your information for advertising or profiling.
  • We do not retain client project data beyond what's needed to deliver and support the work, and never use it for anything outside the agreed engagement.

Opting out

If you received an outreach email from us and don't want any further contact, just reply and say so — we'll stop immediately and won't follow up again. No further action is needed on your part.
